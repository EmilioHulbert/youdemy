Here is how you can manage, delete, and prevent Gemini from saving automatic chats triggered by "Hey Google":

---

### 1. Delete Existing Gemini Chats

You can delete past automatic voice chats directly from your Gemini activity log:

1. Open the **Gemini mobile app** or go to [myactivity.google.com/product/gemini](https://myactivity.google.com/product/gemini) in your browser.
2. Tap your **Profile Picture / Initial** (top right) and select **Gemini Apps Activity**.
3. To delete individual voice chats, tap the **`X`** or **Delete** icon next to specific items.
4. To delete all past chats at once, tap **Delete** near the top of the page and select **All time**.

---

### 2. Disable Saving Gemini Activity Automatically

To stop Gemini from saving your prompts and voice interactions to your chat history altogether:

1. In the **Gemini app**, tap your **Profile Picture / Initial** $\rightarrow$ **Gemini Apps Activity**.
2. Look for the **Keep Activity** toggle at the top (it will say "On").
3. Tap **On** and select **Turn off** (or **Turn off and delete activity**).

> **Note:** When *Keep Activity* is off, future interactions won't be saved in your chat history log or used to build past conversation threads.

---

### 3. Prevent "Hey Google" from Activating Gemini

If you want to keep using the Gemini app manually but stop "Hey Google" voice triggers from automatically opening Gemini and generating new chats:

* **Turn off "Hey Google" for Gemini:**
1. Open the **Gemini app**.
2. Tap your **Profile Picture / Initial** $\rightarrow$ **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Talk to Gemini hands-free**.
3. Toggle off **"Hey Google"**.


* **Switch Back to Google Assistant (Optional):**
1. In the **Gemini app**, tap your **Profile Picture / Initial**.
2. Tap **Switch to Google Assistant**.
