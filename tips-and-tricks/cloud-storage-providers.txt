Finding a "good" amount of free space (50GB+) is getting much harder in 2026 because companies have realized that large free tiers are not sustainable. However, there are a few ways to get more than just 10GB if you are willing to jump through a few hoops or look at niche providers.

### 1. The "Free Tier" Options (50GB+)

* **Degoo:** They are one of the few remaining "generous" providers. They offer a significant amount of free storage (usually starting around 20GB and going up to 100GB via referrals/tasks).
* *The Catch:* Their app is very aggressive about asking you to watch ads or refer friends to "unlock" the full capacity. It is not as polished as Google or pCloud, but for a short-term "dump" of files, it works.


* **Terabox:** This is the "big one" that everyone talks about for free space. They famously offer **1TB of free cloud storage** upon sign-up.
* *The Catch:* They are heavily ad-supported, and their desktop sync client can be intrusive. Many power users use it strictly for "cold" backups (storing files you don't need to open daily) via a browser rather than their sync app.
* *Privacy Warning:* Because they offer so much for free, do not put sensitive personal or work documents there. Use it only for non-critical, encrypted, or redundant backup files.



### 2. The "Short-Term" Strategy (Sign-up Bonuses)

If you just need a temporary place to hold your files while you move things around, you can utilize sign-up bonuses:

* **Dropbox/Box:** They often have small referral programs. If you sign up and then refer a friend (or a second email you control), you can often bump the 2GB/10GB base up to 15–20GB.
* **Sync.com:** They offer a solid 5GB to start, but their referral program is very strong. If you have the patience to refer other accounts, you can scale it up quickly.

### 3. The "Pro" Advice: Use Object Storage (The real deal)

If you have a credit card and want 100GB+ for **dirt cheap**, stop looking for "Free" and look at **Backblaze B2** or **Wasabi**.

* **Why:** You pay for what you use. 100GB of storage on Backblaze B2 costs roughly **$0.60 per month**.
* **The Benefit:** You aren't "paying" for a service; you are paying for the utility of the storage. It is professional, reliable, and you don't have to deal with ads or quota-shrinking "freemium" business models.

**My suggestion for your specific situation:**
If you need 50GB–100GB *immediately* and don't want to pay, **Terabox** is the only one that will give you that much space instantly without needing referrals. Just remember: **treat it like an open public locker.** Never put your only copy of an important file there; treat it as a secondary, "extra" backup location.
